## Summary

Completed days can show “Saved target unavailable” even when their calorie target was saved: Daily Balance reads a single-day response that drops the comparison returned by the calendar. This change returns the validated saved comparison in day reads and phone/watch completion acknowledgements, including their sync payloads. Existing snapshots retain their original target; missing legacy history remains unavailable.

Closes #435. Targets `master`; no unmerged dependency. No data repair, migration or current-target substitution.

## Test plan and behavior evidence

| Scenario | Expected and observed |
| --- | --- |
| Read a completed day with 1,800 kcal intake and a saved 2,000 target | The actual changed serializer and app show **200 kcal remaining**; the actual base shows unavailable. |
| Complete today, refetch and reload while the current plan differs | Three maintained browser cases pass, including switching from the current 2,100 target to saved 2,000 and retaining it after reload. Backend route tests verify acknowledgement, read and sync consistency. |
| Missing legacy snapshot or older payload without the optional field | Remains unavailable after reload. Backend tests also preserve snapshots through goal changes/recompletion and reject partial snapshots and invalid totals. |
